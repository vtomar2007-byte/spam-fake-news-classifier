A NOTE ON ACCURACY

Signals help you ask better questions.

Clearcheck runs a small set of transparent text-pattern checks in your browser. Spam checks look for common pressure tactics and suspicious link patterns. News checks flag language and sourcing cues that can be useful starting points.

It does not browse the web, verify a publisher, inspect a link, or determine whether a claim is true. A low score is not a guarantee of safety or accuracy. Check important claims with reliable, independent sources.
